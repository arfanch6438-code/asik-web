Driver settlement

Hisaab record

Saves this period (the From and To dates above): loads, diesel (Cash and Petro App), cash expenses and total distance. Each load is marked "settled" on the Hisaab date and shows in the Vehicle report. "Settle account to zero" also saves this record by itself.

Diesel and distance

Vehicle meter reading (km)

Enter the meter reading each time you settle with the driver. Total distance = last reading minus the previous reading.

If you pay more than the driver is owed, the extra stays as his advance and is cut from his next settlement automatically.

Al Wasik Vehicles - drivers register

Add driver to a vehicle

The vehicle code becomes "Name-VehicleNo" (e.g. Hamad-3059). It appears in the driver list when you enter loads. Salary is also added in the Salary tab.

Driver resigned / left the vehicle

After this his name no longer appears in the driver list for new loads. Old loads, hisaab and the Vehicle report history stay. His salary stops after that month. To add the new driver, use the form above with the same vehicle no.
